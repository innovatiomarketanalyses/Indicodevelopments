Cross-check against AECOM (Maputo)

AECOM Africa Cost Guide 2025/26, Maputo: average multi unit high rise USD 1 519/m², luxury USD 1 942/m², car park USD 974/m², excluding site works, furniture and equipment. For 3,586 m² + 418 m² covered parking that is MT 374.1 M to MT 471.0 M. Our estimate on the same basis (without site works, furniture, appliances, kitchen and supermarket equipment, pool) is MT 230.7 M, about 38 % below the average figure. The gap is mostly structure and shell (site mixed concrete at inventory prices and assumed Maputo labour rates) and installation labour; both need a contractor's rates. Until quotes arrive, plan with the benchmark range, not the bottom-up number alone. AECOM guide
