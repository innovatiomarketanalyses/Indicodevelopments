How we quote a kitchen or a wardrobe (every job)

  1. Modules from the plan. Measure each run on the plan: tall columns, base units (doors, drawers, sink, hob, dishwasher), wall units, corner, island or peninsula, fillers, plinth, end panels.
  2. Every panel. Each module becomes its panels: sides, bottom, rails, shelves, back, drawer boxes, fronts, plinth, end panels, with the material of each (carcass PVC foam, fronts gloss / Supamatt / oak, backs Unitex).
  3. Whole sheets. Panels are nested onto the real sheet size (2440 x 1220 or 2750 x 1830, 4 mm saw, 10 mm trim). We buy whole sheets. Cutting is free at Mozoasis when we buy the sheets there.
  4. Edge tape and fittings by the piece. Edge tape in metres of every visible edge +10%. Hinges by door height, push latches, runners per drawer, legs, plinth clips, LED + profile + driver, bins, cutlery trays, plate racks, pull outs.
  5. Labour. Crew days x day rate: 0.5 day per cabinet box, 0.6 per wardrobe bay, +1 to set out and finish. Plus delivery and 3% sundries.
  6. Appliances, sink, tap at supplier cost (Makro SA / Game). Stone worktop from the slab nesting (separate line).
  7. Total cost x 2 = client price. The whole kitchen: cabinets, labour, appliances, sink, tap (and stone when we supply it).
