COMMON QUESTIONS
Where exactly is Malongane Beach Villas?

At Ponta Malongane, in Matutuíne district, a few kilometres north of Ponta do Ouro in southern Mozambique, on an 8.2 ha plot of dune forest facing the Indian Ocean.

What stage is the project at?

Concept design. The layout, the villas and the club are fully modelled in 3D; licensing and detailed engineering follow. Your advisor will share the current timeline.

How does ownership work in Mozambique?

Land in Mozambique belongs to the State and is held under a land-use title (DUAT). The ownership structure for buyers is explained by your advisor and set out in the reservation documents.

How do I reserve a villa?

Message your advisor on WhatsApp. You receive the plans, prices and availability, and the next steps for a reservation.
