Privacy Policy

Indico Procurement, operated by Indico Hospitality Holding, Lda. Last updated 19 November 2026.

What this app is

Indico Procurement is an internal tool of Indico Hospitality Holding, Lda ("Indico"). It connects to one company mailbox, procurement.indicodevelopment@gmail.com, so that Indico can send its own procurement emails (requests for quotation and proposals) to suppliers and builders.

What data the app accesses

The app uses Google sign in once, with the Indico procurement mailbox, to get permission to send email from that mailbox. It only handles that mailbox. It does not access the accounts of any other person or customer.

How we use it

The permission is used only to send procurement emails written by Indico. We do not read mail for advertising, we do not sell data, we do not share Google user data with third parties, and we do not use it to train AI models. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Supplier contact details

Business contact details of suppliers (company name, email, phone) are kept to run procurement. Suppliers can ask us at any time to correct or remove their details.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored in Indico's private automation server and are never published. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
