What we need

A 17 apartment building (12 T3, 3 T2, 2 compact T2) plus restaurant, supermarket and club house. Living areas get a large format warm sand porcelain, bedrooms and closets a light oak SPC plank, and kitchens, bathrooms, patios and common areas an anti slip R11 porcelain.
